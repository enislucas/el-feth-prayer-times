# El-Feth Moskee — printed calendar, September 2026 (Rabi' ul Awwal – Rabi' al Akhar 1448)

Typed from the mosque's own printed "Gebedskalender Tilburg en omgeving" sheet
(PRIJS €1,-), photographed by the owner of this app. Local Amsterdam clock
times, exactly as printed.

This file exists as an independent cross-check on the scraped feed. The build
script compares every value against it and refuses to go on quietly if they
disagree by more than a minute.

## Result of the cross-check (22 September 2026)

180 printed values, 179 match the mosque's live feed exactly.

The single exception is **1 September, Isha**: the printed sheet says `22:01`,
both the mosque's website feed and Mawaqit say `22:00`. Mawaqit was suspected of
being wrong; it is not — across the whole of 2026 (2190 values) Mawaqit and the
mosque's own website differ on exactly one value (1 January Isha, 18:29 vs
18:30). The two feeds are the same data.

Where the printed sheet and the feed disagree, **the printed sheet wins** — it
is the calendar physically handed out at the mosque. The build script applies it
as an override and records it in times.json.
